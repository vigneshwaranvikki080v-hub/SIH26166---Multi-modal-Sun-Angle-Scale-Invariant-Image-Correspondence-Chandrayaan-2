Why the Dual-Matcher Architecture?

Lunar surface images captured at different orbital times exhibit extreme shadow reversals. While LoFTR excels at cross-scale viewpoint disparities, RIFT thrives under low-sun shadows because phase congruency computes maximum local energy regardless of illumination variations.
